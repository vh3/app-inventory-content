Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how App Inventory (“the App”), published by Brain Fart Games, handles information. Recognition of screenshots you select is done on your iPhone.

Who we are

Contact: bfgames.support@gmail.com

What stays on your device

  • The Home Screen screenshots you select in the Photos picker
  • The app list, recognition results, and any inventory you keep in the App

The App does not create an account and does not upload those photos or that list in order to recognize apps. Core recognition works without a network connection.

Optional recognition-data download

If you tap Check for updated recognition data, the App downloads a versioned recognition file and a small catalog manifest from this website (vh3.github.io/app-inventory-content). That download is not started on its own. The request does not include your photos, your app list, or a device identifier. If the download fails or does not check out, the App keeps the recognition data it already had.

Optional diagnostics email

If you tap Email Results, the App prepares a zip you can send with Apple Mail. That zip contains the results list and small icon crops. It does not contain the full screenshot. Nothing is sent until you send the message yourself. You can decline and still use the App.

What we do not collect

  • No account, name, or email collected inside the App
  • No advertising identifiers collected by the App for ads
  • No third-party analytics SDK in the current build
  • No sale of personal information

Apple diagnostics

When you install builds through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple may collect crash and usage diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple’s policies. We may review crash reports Apple makes available in App Store Connect. See Apple’s privacy information for those platforms.

Children

The App is not directed at children for data collection. It does not ask for a name, email, or account.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.

Contact

Privacy questions: bfgames.support@gmail.com
