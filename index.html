A list of the apps on your Home Screen

App Inventory is an iPhone app from Brain Fart Games. You choose Home Screen screenshots. The app reads them on your phone and builds a list you can review and export.

No account. Recognition of the photos you select does not require a network connection. An optional check can download a newer recognition file from this website when you ask for it.
